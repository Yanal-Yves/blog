This series grew out of my experience as a developer and software architect: for more than twenty years, I ran into the concrete consequences of **proprietary software** — abandoned technologies, broken compatibility, unilateral changes to pricing or licensing. The founding article starts from these lived situations; this series, still being written, will broaden the discussion to **free software** and **licences**: how they work, what they change for users and organisations, and how to make choices that don't leave you dependent on a single vendor.
